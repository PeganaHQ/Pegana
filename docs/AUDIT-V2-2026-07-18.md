# Pegana V2 — Trust Audit (2026-07-18)

> **The question:** would a competent, skeptical technical buyer at a stablecoin/LST
> issuer, a DeFi protocol, a treasury/market-maker, or an insurer **adopt Pegana and
> trust its verdicts with real money and reputation?**
>
> **Method:** a 7-dimension read-only *adversarial* audit (each dimension hunted the
> concrete reason NOT to trust) over the full workspace, on top of a green validation
> baseline (43 rust suites · 788 web · promtool 26 rules · **byte-exact CLI e2e**).
> Every finding was re-verified 1:1; the real code bugs were fixed + re-validated in
> this same pass. Branch `landing/v2-port`, **not deployed**.

---

## Executive verdict

**The core trust promise is real and holds up under hostile inspection — but a buyer
evaluating the *live* product today is not yet buying it.** Three things are true at once:

1. **The trust invariant is HIGH-confidence and verified by hand.** An independent
   binary (`pegana-replay 0.5.0`) re-derives any receipt to the same RFC-8785 canonical
   hash from typed Decimal fields, and severe transitions are anchored on-chain in an
   SPL Memo signed by a pinned wallet. We traced the whole custody chain (engine →
   onchain_commit → CLI) and re-verified 3 real receipts byte-for-byte this pass. **This
   is the differentiator, and it's genuine, not marketing.**

2. **The engineering discipline is unusually high for the stage** — checked arithmetic
   everywhere (the F-12 class), a centralized "missing data ≠ confirming data" invariant,
   calibration work that visibly self-corrects (the sharkSOL false-positive, the Perena
   window), fail-safe-by-design (a broken input goes UNKNOWN, never a false PEGGED).

3. **But the two things that actually gate adoption are open:** (a) the hardening that
   makes the *live* verdicts trustworthy is **committed but NOT deployed** (prod runs the
   pre-hardening `methodology 0.6.1`), and (b) there is **no demonstrated demand** — no
   paying customer, no live commercial surface, the one organic lead's reply still unsent.

**Verdict: technically trustworthy-by-design, operationally + commercially not-yet-proven.**
The path to "a company would trust this" is short and concrete (below) — it is a
**merge + a handful of ops/GTM actions**, not a rebuild.

## Fixed in this audit pass (real code bugs → all re-validated)

1. **[Formulas — wrong verdict] Transition FSM held PEGGED forever under between-band
   oscillation** (`methodology/src/transition.rs`). A discount alternating between two
   alerting bands (DRIFT↔DEPEG, both stricter than a PEGGED commit) reset the confirm
   timer every tick, so neither band ever confirmed — the engine published **PEGGED while
   the asset was continuously unhealthy**, with no external tell. The ADR-0021/0023 deadband
   only smooths the single PEGGED↔first-band boundary. **Fix:** the window now measures
   continuous time on the *same side* of the committed state, not time at one exact band.
   All prior invariants preserved + a new multi-tick regression test; **byte-exact golden
   + byte-exact CLI e2e** (the fix only touches a previously-unreachable path).
2. **[Security — HIGH] Second SSRF-via-redirect client** (`api-rs/src/main.rs`). The
   dashboard's "send test webhook"/"replay" client lacked `redirect::Policy::none()` (the
   dispatcher has it) — a registered webhook could 302 to loopback/metadata and be followed.
   Reachable by any logged-in user. **Fixed** (mirrors the dispatcher).
3. **[Consistency] Last raw `Decimal /`** (`indexer/sources/pyth.rs` negative-expo scale)
   → `checked_div`, closing the one exception the F-12 sweep left.
4. **[Data hygiene] Garbage `$0` intrinsic rows** (`indexer/sources/hylo.rs`) — hyUSD
   computed with `sol_usd=0` on an empty Pyth cache and wrote a `$0` row for a live
   alerting asset. **Fixed** — skip the tick on missing SOL/USD, matching the other sources.

## What the audit could NOT close here (by design — founder / ops / GTM)

### 🔴 The #1 real-world gap: the hardening is not deployed
Every oracle-trust protection reviewed — the **Pyth confidence gate (ADR-0032)**, the
**intrinsic-liveness gate (ADR-0038)**, ORACLE_DEGRADED/INTRINSIC_DEGRADED, the depth-gate
— is committed on `landing/v2-port` (`methodology 0.7.0`) but **prod runs `0.6.1`**. A buyer
evaluating live verdicts today gets the older build: a Pyth feed can report 500 bps+ of its
own uncertainty with zero effect on the verdict, and a frozen-but-served backing on any LST
is undetected — the exact dSOL failure mode. **This is a merge decision, and it is the
single highest-leverage action available.** (Then: enable the in-hash gates after a fresh
prod re-calibration — they ship inert.)

### Operational maturity (fail-safe today, but not turnkey)
- **No auto-remediation for a dead ingestion task** — the healthz is a dumb TCP echo; a
  dead source sits at UNKNOWN until a human runs `docker compose restart indexer`. MTTR is
  human-latency-bound (solo founder). *Recommend: a watchdog that respawns/pages per-source.*
- **Pyth Hermes has no fallback host** — unlike Solana RPC (real primary→fallback). Hermes
  is the systemic dependency (every USD conversion needs it); an outage stalls the whole
  universe to UNKNOWN. *Recommend: a Hermes mirror/fallback.*
- ~~**`pegana_source_task_up` freezes at 1 on a task *panic*** (vs clean exit).~~ **✅ SHIPPED
  (2026-07-18):** `SourceTaskUpGuard` (`impl Drop` in indexer-rs/src/main.rs) zeroes the
  gauge on any exit including an unwind, so the liveness gauge no longer lies on the panic
  path.
- **The free-public RPC fallback** has no SLA — a correlated-failure risk during exactly the
  network-congestion stress a peg oracle exists to survive.

### Market-leg value-staleness (H12b — still open)
The new nav-accrual-stall (Perena) catches a frozen *intrinsic* backing; the twin for a
frozen *market* leg (a Jupiter/DexScreener route returning HTTP 200 with a value that stopped
advancing — the ONyc/hyloSOL+ ~20h freeze on record) is the deferred **H12b** and remains
open. *The Perena nav-stall pattern is the template to extend to market legs.*

### Architecture: green CI ≠ verified verdict pipeline
- **`engine try_recompute` (685 lines) has no CI-run test** — the one e2e test is gated on
  `PEGANA_E2E_STACK`, which CI never sets, so `cargo test --workspace` goes green without
  ever running the function that decides the verdict + triggers an on-chain spend.
- **The on-chain commit retry/backoff state machine** (spends real SOL, marks `wallet_drained`)
  has **zero tests**. *Recommend: a local-validator or mocked-RPC test for both.*
- **Deepening opportunities** (make the codebase auditable = trustable): fold the ~35
  fine-grained `EngineState` accessors + the "GAP-1/2/3" freeze-order comments into one
  `state.tick(...) -> TickOutcome` that owns the freeze-then-mutate under one lock (makes the
  ordering-bug class *structurally impossible* instead of comment-policed); unify the two
  hand-duplicated webhook-signing contracts (api-rs vs dispatcher-rs) into one `common`
  primitive; move the hand-mirrored `state_reason` SQL string onto the Rust const.

### Accepted trade-offs (documented in ADRs — a buyer should know the scope)
- The **on-chain anchor covers Depeg/Critical/BlackSwan only** (ADR-0004, cost-gated). For
  Drift/Pegged/Unknown — the majority of volume — `pegana-replay` PASS means "internally
  self-consistent hash", not "provably un-swapped". Hash-only tamper-evidence + an
  append-only DB trigger, not a cryptographic guarantee against a privileged actor.
- **`confidence_label` is excluded from re-derivation** (it reads `Utc::now()`): tamper-
  evident, not correctness-verified. Documented, scoped, Option-B follow-up noted.

## Personas, from scratch (who buys this — and what stops them)

| Persona | Job-to-be-done | Biggest thing missing |
|---|---|---|
| **Stablecoin / LST issuer** (Sanctum, Hylo, Perena, Ondo…) | "Prove to *my* users + integrators my peg holds, with third-party evidence I don't control" | The one asset most like theirs (sHYUSD) is self-flagged **miscalibrated / HARD BLOCK**; a live eval fails on the nearest analogue. And the hardening isn't deployed. |
| **DeFi protocol integrating an asset** (lender, perp, LST router) | "Wire an automated action — pause listing, widen a band — to a *reliable* frozen-peg signal" | Operational maturity: no auto-heal, single-host Hermes → the oracle can go dark (safe-UNKNOWN, but *unavailable*) during the exact stress they'd wire against. No paid/metered API tier to build on. |
| **Treasury / market-maker / insurer** | "Independent, replayable peg history to price/hedge/underwrite risk" | Replay is real *for severe events*; for the common Drift/Pegged flow it's hash-only. A no-live-commercial-surface product (pricing 307s to `/`) has no path for them to actually pay. |
| **Retail trust-layer viewer** (best-served today) | "See at a glance if a token I hold is holding its peg, with evidence" | The capability is genuinely there (dashboard, bot, embeddable badge, in-browser verify). What's missing is **demonstrated adoption** — zero cited traffic/subscriber numbers anywhere in the repo. |

**The honest demand read:** Pegana is *technically* the kind of thing these personas should
want, but there is **no evidence any of them has paid or committed**. The one organic,
prospect-generated lead (Ernani / NAV-Guard) has a drafted reply still marked *NOT sent*;
the outreach CRM is still a placeholder; the ADR-0007 launch scorecard is past its own cut
date with empty rows. This is a **go-to-market gap, not a product gap** — and it is squarely
the founder's to close.

## The path to "a company would trust this" (concrete, ordered)

1. **Merge + deploy** the V2 line (the hardening exists; prod is the old build). *Highest leverage.*
2. **Enable the in-hash gates** after a fresh prod re-calibration (they ship inert).
3. **Close the sHYUSD calibration** — it's the asset your issuer pitch stands on.
4. **Ops hardening**: Hermes fallback + a per-source watchdog remain open (the `source_task_up` drop-guard ✅ shipped 2026-07-18).
5. **Test the verdict pipeline in CI** (`try_recompute` + the on-chain commit retry SM).
6. **Ship one commercial surface** (a live `/pricing`, a metered API tier) and **send the Ernani reply.**

None of these is a rebuild. The trust *machine* is built and verified; what remains is
turning it on and putting it in front of a buyer.
