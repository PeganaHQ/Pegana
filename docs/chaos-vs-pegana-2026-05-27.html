Chaos Labs vs Pegana

Date: 2026-05-27. Question: what is the difference between Chaos Labs and Pegana?

Verdict: Chaos Labs is not a direct competitor today. It is the institutional, horizontal risk-oracle platform. Pegana is a narrow, Solana-native peg-risk primitive focused on intrinsic-vs-market state for LSTs, stables, yield-bearing assets, CDPs, and synthetics.

Strategic Read

Chaos validates the category: risk-aware oracles are becoming protocol infrastructure. Pegana should not position as "smaller Chaos Labs." The strongest position is "the specialized peg-state layer Chaos, agents, lenders, insurers, and prediction markets can consume when they need Solana-specific peg semantics."

Risk

Chaos is a substitute today and a vertical-expansion threat later. If Chaos decides to build Solana LST/stablecoin peg semantics directly, Pegana's defensibility depends on speed, public methodology, MCP/webhook distribution, and depth of Solana asset coverage.
